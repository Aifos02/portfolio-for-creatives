Cicatriz

The packaging brings Lanzarote’s story of destruction and rebirth to life through a design that is both symbolic and immersive.

A lid made from volcanic ash recalls the eruption that devastated the island while enriching it for new growth. Bold, striking colors distinguish each salt flavour at a glance, while rounded typography mirrors the soft, organic curves of the lava‑inspired illustrations.

Across the box, lava flows slowly drip downward and transform into local plants, underscoring the idea that catastrophe can give way to regeneration.

Inside, the salt is cradled in a volcano‑shaped container whose circular crater is echoed by the lid’s subtle peek‑through detail, building anticipation and revealing the illustration in stages.

Altogether, this tactile, theatrical experience speaks to diners who value authenticity, rich storytelling, and a deeper connection to nature and resilience.
