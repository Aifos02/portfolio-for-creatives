Upheaval

Upheaval is a portable educational exhibit for high school students (Grades 9–11) that explains why and how earthquakes occur in British Columbia.

Unlike existing safety-focused content, this exhibit addresses the lack of scientific explanation and counters fear-based messaging about the “Big One.” Through 3D graphics, modular design, and clear visuals, the exhibit will make learning engaging, memorable, and accessible across multiple schools.

Problem

British Columbia is one of Canada’s most earthquake-prone regions. While schools emphasize safety drills, most available content focuses on fear of the “Big One” rather than explaining the science behind earthquakes.
