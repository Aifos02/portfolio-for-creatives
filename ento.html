Ento

Ento is a cafe dedicated to creating an inclusive safe space for bug nerds, enthusiasts and for people who feel unwelcome in other “typical” bustling cafes in the city of Vancouver.

Creating connections between bug lovers, who are looking for a chill and quiet environment to casually talk, rest and have a few snacks.
