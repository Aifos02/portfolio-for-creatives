World Map

Created for Capilano University’s Centre for International Experience, this mural reimagines the world as a place of connection rather than borders.

Geometric and organic forms celebrate the distinct cultures and characteristics of countries, while a warm palette of teal and red complements the university’s branding. Together, these elements create a welcoming space where international students can feel included and connected.
