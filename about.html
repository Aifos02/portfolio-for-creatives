  • About Sofia Saienko

    Sofia Saienko is an interdisciplinary designer and artist graduating with a Bachelor of Design from Capilano University's IDEA School of Design. With six years of formal art education at Yalta Children's Art School named after F. A. Vasiliev, she developed a strong foundation in drawing, painting, composition, and art history.

    Alongside her design practice, Sofia has over a year of experience teaching ceramics to children at Evelia Espinosa Art Studio, helping young artists explore materials, develop new skills, and express their creativity.

    Sofia enjoys experimenting with different mediums and creative processes, with interests spanning branding, visual storytelling, illustration, environmental design, and hands-on artistic practices.
